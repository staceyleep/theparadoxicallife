Services

Ways we can work together

Whether you come as an individual, as a couple or as a leader, our work begins by understanding where you have come from.

Therapy

Individual therapy

A steady, confidential space to work through anxiety, burnout, grief, identity questions and life transitions. Using inner child work and IoPT, we look for the source of what is troubling you, and tend to it there.

Relationships

Couples therapy

Understand the patterns that each of you carries into the relationship, learn to hear and be heard, and build a bond that holds both closeness and individuality.

Leaders

Leadership coaching

For executives and C-suite leaders working on personal presence, personal effectiveness and the wise use of self, so you can lead from a healthy centre rather than from exhaustion.

Groups

Speaking, training & organisations

Talks and workshops on trauma, diversity and interpersonal dynamics, and support for organisations seeking healthier human systems.

Getting started

What to expect

  1. Say hello

    Message me on WhatsApp or email, or book a time directly. There is no need to have it all figured out.

  2. Your first session

    I explain the stance I hold as your therapist or coach, answer your questions, and we map your family genogram together.

  3. Mapping the work

    From your genogram I read your functioning template, and we agree where the wounds and pain points are and where to begin.

Sessions

Session lengths

Choose the length that suits the work you want to do.

60 min

A focused session for steady, ongoing work.

90 min

Extra room for deeper work, such as genogram mapping and inner child work.
