The paradoxical life

Both/and, not either/or. Breathing in is as important as breathing out.

Strength and vulnerability. Ambition and rest. Closeness and independence. Wisdom grows when we learn to hold two opposing truths at once, rather than forcing ourselves to choose.

My approach

Healing at the source

Rather than managing symptoms one by one, I seek to work with where they began. When the original wound is attended to, the effects often ripple through a person's work, relationships and sense of self.

Inner child work

Meeting the younger parts of you that learned how to cope, and offering them what they needed then.

Identity-Oriented Psychotrauma Theory

A way of understanding trauma through the splitting of identity, and of finding the way back to your healthy self.

Your family map

A family genogram helps us see how you came to be the way you are, so our work can begin in the right place.
