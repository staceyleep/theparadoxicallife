Community

Empowering Self-Healing

"The best project you will ever work on is you." Join a supportive space where we explore different therapeutic modalities and share monthly activities such as walks and book clubs. It is for people who have been doing self-work with a professional and want a community of like-minded people.

We screen applicants to keep this a safe space for everyone. To join, send me a message and tell me a little about your self-work so far and what you hope to gain and contribute.
